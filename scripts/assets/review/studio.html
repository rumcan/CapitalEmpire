Sprite studio

Every sprite is shown at the size the game draws it, and your pointer is a townsperson at the same scale (feet on the pointer): stand him at a door to check it. Smaller / Bigger change the sprite everywhere it is placed.

Click a sprite in the lot. Alt-click picks the ground decal under the click instead of the upright.
Click a frame to fix it: mirror it, use another frame (of any facing) in its place, or mark it as needing a new frame and drop the new picture in. Rebuild game sprites to put the fixes in the game. The strip on the right plays the walk as the game does (frames 1-8).
