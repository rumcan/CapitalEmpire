## Description (the page body)

**Fairway Fortune** is a golf club builder about the money you manage while you build it.

You start with **$75,000** and an old driving range for sale by a country road: a dirt car park, four mats and an outfield full of junk. Buy it, and golfers start turning in for a bucket of balls. That small, steady income is your business.

**Every decision is a money decision.**
- **Build what you like, in any order.** The Build menu shows every option with a picture, its price, its monthly upkeep and when it pays for itself.
- **Set your prices.** A higher bucket price earns more per golfer and brings fewer of them.
- **Save or borrow.** Click your cash to open the Bank: see the monthly payment and the *total interest* before you sign, repay early, or put cash in savings.
- **Read your month.** Each month ends with a statement: what came in, what went out, and what your club is worth.

**Then play the course you built.** "Play this hole" opens the exact hole you shaped in a painted, behind-the-golfer golf view: hold to swing, click the ground to aim, club up from the rough.

**Practise on your own range.** Hit balls at the target greens and the junk on the outfield, or chip and putt: six drills unlock shot shaping, flight control and the putt read, and lower your handicap.

### How to play
- Mouse: the tool rail on the left; click your cash for the Bank; the Build button for everything you can build.
- Golf view: hold the swing button (or Space) and let go; click the ground to aim; 1-9 or the wheel for clubs; Esc to leave.
- Tip: the speed buttons at the top run time faster while your range earns.

### Financial literacy (Zoud GameLab, "Make FinLit Fun")
No quizzes and no lectures: you learn by running the business. Pricing against demand, payback on an investment, saving versus borrowing, the real cost of a loan, and the difference between profit, cash and net worth all show up in your own numbers as you play. The design document explains the learning outcomes.
