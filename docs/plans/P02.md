# P02: M2 Crowds, finish and verify (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **draft for the owner's approval** (second lead, 2026-10-24; M2 handed over by Argon and the owner). Issue #65.

## Where we are (verified in the code on main, 2026-10-24)
- **Landed and browser-checked** (docs/NOW.md): M2.1 gates (walkers queue at the placed gates, admitted at 600 fans a lane an hour), M2.2 galleries (admitted fans stand at the tees, greens and stands), M2.3 going home (the fans walk back and their cars drive home), M2.4 results from the movers (gate wait, late, missed and exit P95 are measured when gates are placed).
- **Two time scales that don't agree.**
  - The tournament's movers assume **1 sim s = 1 game minute**: the gate rate (`GATE_LANE_PER_HOUR` → 10 a lane a sim s), the 600 s surge, the 120 s leave delay, and calendar.ts `GATES_OPEN` (420), `FIRST_TEE` (450), `LAST_TEE`, `PLAY_END` (1110) and `EXIT_END` (1170). FIRST_TEE, LAST_TEE and PLAY_END are not used anywhere.
  - The town day (M1) runs 600 sim s for 24 hours, so 1 sim s is 2.4 game minutes. Nothing in the tournament reads the town hour: the tournament starts at whatever hour the season ends.
  - The tournament as it plays (departures 0–330 s, kick-off 600 s, leaving 720–1020 s, walks of several hundred s) is longer than a whole town day.
- **The town keeps running** during the tournament: the commutes and lot trips go on (sim.ts `lotTrips.step`), which is right. Growth and visit cars pause because the season clock is frozen.
- **The crowd:** tier 1 at rep 30 is 390 fans; the $5 parking fee cuts that to 360 = 120 party cars, all from the town's homes. In the browser checks only 168 arrived: the rest were stuck at the junctions, turned away or stranded. Those are physical limits (M3's subject), not a cap.
- **Still formulas:**
  - viewing is a constant 0.85 (yearend.ts `DEFAULT_VIEWING`);
  - tents, food and drink spread the arrived crowd over the greens by a catchment weight (eventtool.ts `evaluateEventToolDay`);
  - days 2+ and the practice days come from `resolveMultiDayWithPractice`;
  - without placed gates, the gate flows are the old curve (`measureGateFlows`).
- **Galleries never move:** a fan stays at their first spot until leaving.

## Decisions (proposed)
1. **The tournament day is one town day at 1 game minute per sim s.** When the tournament starts, the town day switches to a 1440 s day and re-phases so the clock reads **05:00**. Every existing tournament constant then becomes true clock time: gates 07:00, first tee 07:30, play ends 18:30, exits by 19:30. At the end (19:30, or when the last car is out, whichever is later; capped at 22:00) the town day returns to the player's `dayLen` and carries on from that hour. At 8× speed the day takes about 3 real minutes; at 1× about 24.
2. **A timeline drives the movers**, replacing the fixed surge offsets. A pure module (`golf/daytimeline.ts`) gives:
   - **departures:** each party leaves home at its wanted arrival time minus a travel estimate (its home's road distance at the average town speed). Wanted arrivals follow the existing `arrivalShare` curve (07:00–13:00, peak about 10:00);
   - **gates:** closed until `GATES_OPEN`; early fans queue (the queue shows the morning build-up);
   - **leaving:** each party's wanted leave time follows `exitShare` (from 16:00 to `PLAY_END`), replacing `LEAVE_AFTER` / `LEAVE_S`;
   - **late:** a fan admitted after `FIRST_TEE` + 45 min (`ON_TIME_GRACE_MIN`) is late. That replaces today's "waited over 45 min at the gate", which can't see a fan who arrived at noon.
3. **Galleries follow the play.**
   - The featured groups move through the holes from `FIRST_TEE` to `LAST_TEE` and finish by `PLAY_END`. A spot's draw at a minute is its base weight × (1 + a bump while the featured group is at that hole).
   - Every 30 game minutes a share of each spot's fans (25%) walks to the spot with the most room for its draw, as a stage-3 walker between spots.
   - The 18th green fills in the afternoon. Tents and stands keep a steady share.
4. **Results from the movers for day 1.**
   - **Viewing** = the share of fan-minutes inside spent at a spot under its capacity; over capacity counts as poor viewing.
   - **Tents, food and drink** = fan-minutes at each tent's spot × that tent type's spend per hour (the same money table `tentDay` uses), replacing the catchment spread.
   - The per-structure counts reach the results card.
   - Days 2+ stay formulas, scaled from day 1's measured facts (decision for Beta; see Not in Alpha).
5. **A tournament-day strip in the HUD** (only while the tournament runs): the clock, markers at gates / first tee / last tee / exits, and live counts (on the road, parked, queuing, inside, at the stands, leaving). It is the minimal HUD M2 needs; P04 restyles the season HUD around it.

## Risks
- Real time: a 1440 s day is about 24 real minutes at 1×. The skip-to-tournament flow should drop to 8× by default for the day (the owner's call).
- More fans walking between spots means more movers: `WALK_CAP` is 6000 groups, and today's 360 fans are far below it. The bench must stay inside the GAME-PLAN 2.3 budgets at tier-5 crowds (P03 brings thousands from the highway).
- Saves taken mid-tournament restore as "free" today (round.ts). T048 must keep that safe: the town day falls back to the normal `dayLen`.
