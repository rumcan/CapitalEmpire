# P22: Start with one road and the driving range; the town grows outward

Status: **decided by the lead under the owner's standing rule** (2026-10-24, recorded in docs/LEAD.md section 5). Issue #148 (T083).
Written from `plan/P22-range-start` at `e7e5491` (main after #139). Research only: no code changed.

The owner (2026-10-24): "New addition to the game. The game starts with nothing but a road passing a driving range. The town grows from
your driving range outward. No more prepainted streets for the town."

## 0. The plan on one screen

- **The start:** one county road crosses the map edge to edge (class `road`, two map exits). Beside it is the player's **driving range**,
  a new golf business, on an owned parcel big enough for a first nine. There are no town streets, no lots, no old nine and no clubhouse.
- **The town has a seed, not streets.** One town centre is seeded across the road from the range. The existing town planner lays every
  street itself. Its first gravel street goes in during the first game hour, opposite the range.
- **Grown-town rules** (range starts only):
  - the town grows faster than today's golf town and stays compact round the range;
  - its pace rises with the holes the player builds and with tournament success;
  - its roads and lots never enter the course land.
- **Early money and life come from the road:**
  - passing traffic between the two exits;
  - drivers from both exits pulling into the range (its takings);
  - $600,000 start money, as today.
- **No tournament until the course is ready.** Founding seasons end with a short report. The tour invites the player once tier 1's needs
  are met: 9 holes, a car park, and a clubhouse the player now places.
- **Old saves keep their towns.** Only new saves carry `golf.start: 'range'`; everything new is gated on it. The old start stays behind
  `?start=town` for comparisons and tests.
- **Checks:** a new `range:gate` grows the town for 20 seasons and holds it to bands. It also writes a grown-town fixture for the bench and
  manual checks. The existing gates are unchanged.

### 1.2 How the town grows today
- **The road AI.**
  - The planner runs in the sim worker. `City.planGrow` (city.ts:1682-1700) runs at most once a game hour. With town centres it lays at
    most one leg every `GRAVEL_EVERY_H` = 6 game hours (event/config.ts:117-118).
  - The trigger is a shortage of land, not a timer: `needsLand` → `short()` (lots/roads.ts:99-108). It counts the reachable EMPTY lots
    within `100 + 10 × sqrt(built)` tiles of the built centre, against `max(24, 1.5 × (20 + 0.1 × built))`.
  - `planExtension` (roads.ts:233-242) scores candidate legs and lays the best valid one. Candidates are dead ends continued, corners'
    missing arms, and branches every 40 tiles off reachable `gravel | lane | street | road` segs (`BRANCH_IDS`, roads.ts:36). The score is
    the distance to the centre plus penalties. Legs snap to the 40-tile lattice.
  - With centres, a leg never touches a road outside `BRANCH_IDS` (roads.ts:116, :202).
  - The engine lays the leg as a free gravel town road with a reveal animation and toasts "The town laid a gravel road" (engine.ts:2515-2525,
    wired at :756).
- **Lots.**
  - `segLots` cuts frontage lots on both sides of every ground seg except `noLots` course drives (lots/lots.ts:185-221). Sizes by class
    are at lots.ts:26: gravel/street 10 x 15, road 12.5 x 16, avenue 15 x 18.
  - Enclosed blocks are subdivided (lots/blocklots.ts:74-176).
  - Updates are incremental: the edit box is padded by 54 tiles (lots.ts:234-291).
- **The Town model** (lots/town.ts) balances target shares: res 0.65, com 0.2, ind 0.1, tour 0.05 (town.ts:40).
  - Lot weight: `zoneGrowth(access) × exp(-d/40) × lotValue` (town.ts:221-255). `d` is the distance to the nearest built lot, which keeps
    growth contiguous.
  - With centres the town grows only `(5 + 0.02 × built)` gravel lots a game day (town.ts:198; config.ts:110-113). Paved frontage adds
    `PAVED_RATE` = 6 lots a game hour (town.ts:201-205; config.ts:114-115). That rate covers any non-gravel lot, town road or not.
  - A lot builds in half a game day (town.ts:182). A civic lot comes every 400 residents (town.ts:314).
- **Districts** (lots/districts.ts).
  - Commercial: asphalt frontage within 100 tiles of the centre (districts.ts:7-21).
  - Industrial: the 80 x 80 corner of the 8 x 8 grid nearest the entry (districts.ts:24-44). Today it assumes the pre-laid grid.
- **Lot looks:** P20 is merged. `compose` draws every lot from `lot-presets.json` by use, state and size bucket, with the nearest-bucket
  fallback (lots/compose.ts:168-172, :286; lots/presets.ts). Grown lots need nothing new.
- **Speed.** A game hour is 60 sim s and a game day is 1,440 sim s (city.ts:51; town.ts:31). In golf mode one game day is a whole season
  (golf/calendar.ts:18-24), so **the town gets 24 growth hours a season**, 24 real minutes at 1x. Dev builds run growth at x20
  (engine.ts:751).
- **After a tournament,** `closeRound` → `growTowns(rate)` stamps existing EMPTY lots to `pop × (1 + rate)` at once (city.ts:459-485;
  golf/towngrowth.ts:52-118). No roads come with it; the rate is 1-6% plus 2% per tier above 1.
- **The course land.**
  - Lots never start on it: `reserved()` (city.ts:309-324) uses `golfLand()` (engine.ts:372). Each hour `clearCourse` empties the course
    rect (city.ts:327-331).
  - **Town roads are not kept off it.** The planner's `blocked` (city.ts:264-273) covers the site, businesses, car parks, rows, tolls and
    stops only.
  - `short()` counts golf-land lots as free land (roads.ts:103), which can stall growth.

## 2. Decisions

### 2.1 The start state
1. **The county road runs edge to edge, with two map exits.**
   - **Class:** `road`, the two-lane rural road. It is in `BRANCH_IDS`, so the town may branch off it (roads.ts:36). Its lots are
     12.5 x 16, and it counts as commercial frontage near the centre.
   - It is laid as a **town road**: free and with no upkeep, because it is the county's (city.ts:370).
   - **Route:** a straight axis-aligned line through the range's junction J. It is straight for at least 600 tiles each side of J, so the
     town's 40-tile lattice meets it square. Beyond that, a half may take one 45° bend to reach a better edge.
   - It crosses water by at most 2 bridges, never tunnels, and never ends on a mountain edge.
   - Candidates are scored by structure cost, as `highwayRoute` does (structures.ts:92-103).
   - **The exits** are the two border points. They are saved (`golf.exits`) and named after off-map towns (`Names(seed).town(1)` and
     `(2)`), with signs at the border, for example "Lowell Junction 18 mi".
   - *Why two exits:* "a road passing" means through traffic. Two ends give two sources and sinks from minute one, and P03's highway crowd
     a place to enter.
2. **The site** (pure `planRangeStart`).
   - **J:** candidates are the terrain's three town sites (generate.ts:430-459), nearest the map centre first, then a 64-tile scan near the
     centre. J is snapped to the 40-tile lattice.
   - **Layout,** in the road's frame (s along the road, t across; +t is the course side; V = road half width + walk + 2):
     - **Parcel (owned):** 300 tiles along the road × 450 deep, s ∈ [J−70, J+230], t ∈ [V, V+450]. This is today's course size
       (place.ts:13), about 54 ha: room for a nine, not for 18.
     - **Range plot:** 70 × 150 tiles (140 × 300 m, a real range's size) in the parcel's road corner, s ∈ [J−70, J]. Golfers hit away from
       the road.
     - **Range front** (the business rect): the plot's first 24 tiles. It holds the hut (the four-view `business:golf-driving-range`,
       about 16 x 10), the covered mats (12 bays) and a 20 x 16 car park (about 20 bays).
     - **Drive:** a `drive-gravel` leg (P19's course drive, network.ts:68) of at most 20 tiles from the road into the car park.
     - **Town seed:** the town centre, 40 tiles across the road from J (far side).
   - **Fit rules:**
     - the parcel is ≥ 98% GROUND/PATCH/HILL (as `COURSE_BUILD`);
     - the range front and car park are fully buildable;
     - a town window of 600 × 400 tiles across the road is ≥ 75% buildable;
     - all of it lies ≥ 64 tiles (`SITE_MARGIN`) from the map edges.
   - **Fallbacks:** the smaller course sizes, then up to 8 seeds, then the old start.
3. **What is built at minute zero:**
   - the county road, the drive, the range (business, props and ground) and the owned-land outline;
   - farms along the road far from the seed. `ext/farms` places them on segs more than 300 tiles from a centre (farmmap.ts:9, :57-64),
     which gives a rural road;
   - lazy forest on hills (forest.ts:77-100);
   - nothing else: no lots, no stamp, no old nine, no clubhouse, no event structures.
4. **The camera and the first tool.**
   - The camera is centred on the range hut at z 0.17 (the street tier).
   - The tool is **Course** with its panel open on **New hole** (P21's wizard). Until P21's wizard is ticketed and lands, it opens on
     today's stage-1 Tee brush (progressive-ui/index.ts:84).
   - Progressive UI stage 1 as today (index.ts:72-84).
5. **Money: $600,000, as today** (city.ts:502).
   - The old nine was free. Building a nine at seed prices costs about $300,000: per hole, a fairway of about 12,000 m² at $2, a green of
     550 m² at $6, a tee of 300 m² at $3 and about 6,000 m² of rough at $0.8 (costs.ts:14-22).
   - That leaves about $300,000 for the clubhouse, the car park and tier-2 preparation. P05 tunes it.
   - The client mirror's $80,000 flash (sim/client.ts:145) is fixed.
   - In range starts the career milestones pay as **county development grants**: $10k, $20k, $30k and $50k at 250, 1,000, 2,500 and
     4,000 residents (career.ts:23-28). The town's growth is now the player's achievement.

### 2.2 Where early demand and money come from
1. **The driving range is a visit business** like the clubhouse: game-placed and outside `BUSINESSES`.
   - **Type values:** id `range`, footprint 70 x 24 (the front), hours 7-22 (floodlit), party 2, rate 80, tau 8, upkeep $2,000 a game
     day (a season).
   - **Price:** $60 a visitor. One sim car stands for five parties at a $6 bucket. This follows P03's `CAR_SCALE` idea: the car park stays
     lively without flooding the road.
   - **Year-1 target:** about 150 cars a season, about $18,000 in takings. P05 tunes it.
2. **The range draws from both exits (passers-by) and from the town's homes.**
   - The tourist catchment (city.ts:1453-1462) is generalised from one `ctx.entry` to every exit in `ctx.exits`.
   - The range draws `RANGE_PASS_POP(tier)` = 1,500 × [1, 1.3, 1.6, 2, 2.4] per exit. Tourism and the clubhouse keep drawing 300 per
     level from every exit.
   - Visits plan with zero residents.
3. **Through traffic.** A small pure generator sends cars from one exit to the other. They have no stop and are despawned at the far
   border.
   - **Volume:** 240 cars a town day at the start, shaped by the town-day curve (`demandAt`, trips.ts:35), times (1 + residents/2,000),
     times [1, 1.2, 1.5, 1.8, 2.2] by tier.
   - It makes the road alive in minute one and, later, a real load on the town's junctions (M3).
   - It is cheap: about 80 cars on a 4 km road at the start, about 2,000 a town day at tier 5.
4. **The tournament crowd from off the map** (range starts).
   - Town fans are at most 25% of the residents. The rest of the tier's crowd enters at the exits, using P03's highway split (T055/T056).
   - Without this, tier 1's 390 fans would strand in a town of 150 people.
   - The old start keeps P03's shares (tier 1 all local).

### 2.3 The growth rules (range starts only; old saves keep today's)
1. **Where the town starts.**
   - The planner is short of land at minute one. The road frontage within 100 tiles of the seed gives about 14 lots on the far side and a
     sliver on the near side outside the parcel, under `short()`'s 24-30.
   - So the **first gravel street goes in at the first growth hour**, branching off the county road at J on the far side. The first house
     goes up on the road frontage the same hour.
2. **How it chooses where to grow next.** The existing planner and Town picks, with three changes:
   - **Anchored centre:** the planner's centre is the midpoint of the seed and the built lots' mean, not the mean alone (city.ts:1388-1392).
     The town's heart stays at the range.
   - **Compact rings:** a lot's pick weight gains `exp(-dc / (120 + 12 × sqrt(built)))`, with dc its distance to the anchored centre. Lots
     fill along roads (the existing `exp(-d/40)` contiguity) inside a radius that grows with the town, so the county road never ribbons out
     for kilometres.
   - **The town's own lots grow at the town's rate:** lots on town segs of any class (the county road and its gravel streets) and any
     gravel lot. `PAVED_RATE` (6 an hour) applies only to lots on the player's own paved roads. Paving still pays, but the county road is
     not a free 144-lots-a-season strip.
3. **Speed: the town's lots a season.** `(GROWN_BASE 30 + GROWN_PER_HOLE 3 × min(holes, 18) + GROWN_PER_BUILT 0.04 × built) × success`,
   spread over the season's 24 growth hours.
   - **success** is 1 in founding seasons. After each tournament it is `clamp(0.7 + 8 × roundTownGrowthRate, 0.7, 1.8)`
     (golf/towngrowth.ts:52-85): a bad year slows the town; tier 3 with satisfaction ≥ 80 gives about 1.5.
   - This **replaces the post-tournament stamp**, so growth is always seen happening.
   - **Roads:** the planner may lay a leg every game hour (as the single town does), not every 6.
   - **Build time:** a lot builds in 2 game months (240 sim s), not half a season.
   - **First lot:** the accumulator starts at 1, so the first lot starts in the first growth hour.
4. **The town's shape** (expected with a steady player: 2 holes in season 1, a nine by season 2, 18 by season 6, success 1.0-1.3; about 2.3
   residents a lot):

   | When | Lots | Residents | What it looks like |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Year 1 | ~35 | ~80 | A hamlet: houses facing the range across the road, a corner shop or garage on the road (commercial frontage), one or two short gravel streets off the road at J |
   | Year 5 | ~290 | ~650 | A village of about 4 × 4 blocks (160 × 160 tiles) on the far side; the road is main street with shops near J; two civic lots; a few houses on the course side beyond the parcel's ends |
   | Year 20 | ~2,500 | ~5,500 | A town about 560 × 560 tiles (1.1 km) across, mostly on the far side, wrapping round the parcel's ends; shops on the player's paved roads; an 80 × 80 industrial yard toward the nearer exit; the course land an untouched green block |

   For scale: today's start town (1,000 residents) is about year 6-7. The constants are starting values; T098's gate holds them to the
   bands in section 2.7.
5. **It never crowds or blocks the course land.**
   - **Lots:** `reserved()` and `clearCourse` already keep them off (city.ts:309-331).
   - **Roads (new, every golf game):** the planner's `blocked` gains the golf land padded by 6 tiles. Town roads can cross the course
     today: this is a bug fix.
   - **Land count (new, every golf game):** `short()` counts only lots that can build. Golf-land lots no longer stall growth.
   - **Breathing room (range starts):** a candidate leg whose midpoint is within 40 tiles of golf land scores +40. The town grows away from
     the land the player is likely to buy.
   - **Buying land** that the town has built on is unchanged: a bought block's houses go at the empty-land price (GOLF-PLAN 4.4).
6. **Districts in range starts.**
   - **Commercial:** asphalt frontage within 100 tiles of the centre, as today (districts.ts:7-21). The county road near J becomes main
     street.
   - **Industrial:** an 80 × 80 rect on the town side, 200-280 tiles from the seed toward the nearer exit. It opens at 250 built lots.

### 2.4 Other towns and the regional network
1. **No other towns on the map.** The game is one town (GOLF-PLAN 3). The off-map world is the two exits: their names, signs, through
   traffic and visitors. The terrain's other two town sites stay empty land. The classic valley stays for legacy saves and `?start=valley`
   (dev).
2. **No towns seeded by growth in P22.** A hamlet at a far junction (hotels, tier 4) is an idea for later, not planned here.
3. **The regional network grows with the tiers.** P03's entries 2 and 3 (tier 3 and tier 4) become real county roads in range starts.
   - At the tier-3 verdict, the county lays a new `road` from a third map edge to the town's nearest node, with the toast "The county
     opened a road to X".
   - At the tier-4 verdict it lays another one from the fourth edge.
   - This is T097, the form P03's T057 takes in range starts.

### 2.5 The golf course grows from the range; the tier and tournament flow
1. **Owned at start:** the 300 × 450 parcel, with the range in its road corner.
   - The course grid (P21 T078) covers it; its `allow` refuses the range plot. Holes are built with P21's tools (the New hole wizard and the
     cell brush, once ticketed and landed; today's Course > Build until then).
   - 18 holes need bought blocks (golf/parcels.ts:144-162; engine.ts:3149), as today.
2. **The clubhouse is a building the player places** (Course > Build > Buildings): one only, $45,000, 40 × 24.
   - It sits on a course drive's verge (P19 frontage snap, ext/frontage-snap, T069a merged).
   - Placing it makes it the venue: `golf.clubhouse` and the site the crowd walks to move to it. Until then the range front is the venue
     plot.
   - Green fees (the clubhouse visit business) start then.
   - Old saves keep their game-placed clubhouse.
3. **Founding seasons:** no tournament until the tour invites.
   - **Range starts' tier-1 needs:** 9 holes, a car park (the range's counts) and the clubhouse.
   - **The invitation** comes the moment the needs are met: a toast and the HUD chip "The Club Championship: end of this season". The
     tournament is held at that season's end if the invitation came at least 3 months before, else at the next season's end.
