# P03: M3 Transport and roads on the day (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **draft for the owner's approval** (second lead, 2026-10-24; M3 handed over by Argon and the owner). Issue #66. Builds on P02 (the tournament on the town day) and on Argon's highway-entries note (Drive, copied to the lead's temp folder; its line numbers are against snapshot v16).

## Where we are (verified on main, 2026-10-24)
- **The levers that exist:**
  - car parks with fees (`parkingfee.ts`: a fee cuts demand);
  - bay rows and on-street runs (`event/rows.ts`, `event/curb.ts`);
  - shuttle routes from a car park to the gates (`event/bus.ts`), counted as park and ride by `parkride.ts` when the car park is ≥ 80 tiles from the clubhouse;
  - toll gates;
  - road building and junction types.
- **The crowd comes only from the town's homes.** The map's highway entry (`terrain.highway`) feeds only the off-season tourists (`City.planVisits`). At tier 1, 360 fans = 120 party cars came, and only 168 fans arrived in the checks: junction jams, full car parks and unreachable homes.
- **The crowd size by tier** (tour.ts `TIERS`): 300–600, 1.5–2.5k, 4–8k, 12–25k and 30–50k spectators. At 3 fans a car, tier 5 is 10,000–17,000 cars, beyond what the sim can move in a tournament morning on top of the town (the GAME-PLAN 2.3 budgets are set around 10k cars in total).
- **Road closures as a mechanism exist:** the disasters close segments by blocking their lanes (`City.closed`, `applyClosures`), and the routing avoids them. There is no player-facing closure, one-way for the day, police or drop-off.
- **Jams in the results:** P02's measured facts give the gate wait, late, missed and exit times. The per-source counters (sent, parked, turned away, lost, stranded, on the road) exist (`Surge.result`), but the results card doesn't say *why* fans were late or missed.

## Decisions (proposed)
1. **The seasonal crowd enters by the highway** (Argon's design, verified against today's code):
   - share by tier 0 / 0.55 / 0.70 / 0.80 / 0.85 from the highway (tier 1 stays local);
   - the crowd splits `[town, entry 1, entry 2, …]` and reuses the per-source counters;
   - spawns sit on **every inbound lane** of the border segment (`City.entryDrivesAt`, inbound DSeg only, staggered offsets 3 / 14 / 25 m);
   - a 2nd highway entry opens at tier 3 and a 3rd at tier 4 (border road stubs on other map edges, marked on the map).
   Departures follow P02's timeline: highway cars "leave home" at wanted arrival minus the drive from the border.
2. **One sim car can stand for several.** From tier 3, each surge car carries `CAR_SCALE` parties: 1 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 by tier. Its bay count, gate admissions and fan counts multiply. The car occupies one road slot but counts as that many in the car park's fill and in the results. This keeps the tier-5 morning near 2,500–3,000 sim cars. It's the only way the GAME-PLAN budgets hold. The bench gates it: run at tier 5 before T057 lands.
3. **The event traffic plan:** a "Day plan" tab in the Event panel, with three tools that act only from 05:00 to 20:00 on tournament days:
   - **Close a road** (click segments): the disaster closure blocks, applied for the day. Through traffic and the crowd route round it.
   - **One way for the day** (click a road, pick the direction; *tidal*: inbound until 13:00, outbound after): blocks the other direction's lanes, the same block mechanism.
   - **Police** (place on a junction or a car-park entrance, $ per officer per day): at a junction it holds the cross traffic for the event flow (a priority override on the lights or stop rule); at a car-park entrance it raises the intake rate (×1.5), so queues on the road shrink.
   - **Drop-off** is proposed for Beta: it needs taxis and the hotels (after the MVP).
   - The plan saves in the golf save (`dayPlan`), shows as overlays (red closed segs, arrows, police icons) and can be edited any time before or during the day.
4. **Jams reach the results, with reasons.** The results card gets a "Why fans were late or missed" block. Each count is measured, and each names the fix:
   - *still on the road at first tee*, with the worst junctions from the bottleneck report → "Upgrade or police these junctions";
   - *turned away (car parks full)* → "Add bays or park and ride";
   - *stranded (no road)* → "Connect …";
   - *lost*;
   - *late at the gate* → "More gate lanes".
   A new **Traffic** satisfaction line: the mean car trip time against the free-flow estimate, from 100 at ≤ 1.2× down to 0 at ≥ 3×. Angry fans spend less in the tents, with the same scale.
5. **A pre-day forecast** in the Event panel, so the player can act before the day:
   - expected cars by source and hour;
   - car-park capacity against expected cars;
   - shuttle seats per hour against the park-and-ride share;
   - the gate lanes' hourly capacity against the peak arrivals.
   Each line is red when it won't cope. It's computed from the same pure timeline as the day (P02 T049), so the forecast and the day agree.

## Not in Alpha
- Drop-off, taxis and hotel shuttles (after-MVP list), message signs, and train or coach terminals (the tier-5 `coachTerminal` need stays a structure).
- A full contraflow lane model: tidal one-way blocks whole directions.

## Risks
- **Sim load at tiers 4–5:** CAR_SCALE plus the bench gate. If the town plus a scaled crowd still breaks the budget, the next lever is fewer town commuters on tournament days (people take the day off), not a bigger scale.
- **Spawn headway:** one lane admits about 220–260 cars in 330 s (Argon). With every inbound lane and the staggered offsets, a 4-lane highway takes about 1,000 in the window. With CAR_SCALE 6 that's 6,000 parties.
- **Closures that cut the town off:** a closure plan that strands homes. The forecast flags stranded homes; the day plan warns before saving.
