# P04: M4 The season loop, tidy (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **draft for the owner's approval** (lead, 2026-10-24). Issue #67.

## Where we are (verified in the code on main, 2026-10-24)
- **The year runs:** the game day is the season (calendar.ts: 12 months of 120 sim s from September, the tournament at the end
  of August). The HUD shows the tier, the month and "N months to the tournament" (App.tsx `golfView`). **Skip to tournament**
  fast-forwards to the end of the day (engine `skipToTournament`); P02 makes it set 8× for the day.
- **Results and next season exist:** the results card (App.tsx `RoundCard`) shows the arrival share, the town growth line, each
  town's counts, the money lines, where they got stuck (with Go), the multi-day line, and the 1-of-3 card draft. `closeRound`
  applies the year end: tier, reputation, poor years, the business income and the upkeep; the town grows (city.ts
  `roundTownGrowthRate`).
- **Gaps against GOLF-PLAN 2 and 8:**
  - **The tier offer is taken automatically** (yearend.ts "Automatically accept tier offer"). GOLF-PLAN 2.3 says the player may
    stay.
  - **Nothing warns before a demotion.** `poorYears` is counted and saved, but the player first hears of it when demoted.
  - **The next tier's checklist** is computed (coursestats.ts `missingForNext`, `missingLabels`) but only inside the Course stats
    panel, which the player has to open.
  - **Off-season money is booked once, at the results** (`computeTournamentCloseBook`: green fees, memberships, upkeep). The
    treasury does not move with the months, so a player can't see the year's money coming in or run low mid-year.
  - **Grow-in is silent:** seeded greens need 6 months, but nothing warns when a surface will not be ready by the tournament,
    and Skip does not ask.
  - **Tournament week is one jump:** practice days and days 2+ are resolved inside the results (`resolveMultiDayWithPractice`);
    the player never sees the week as days.
  - **No "what next":** four first-run hints, then nothing tells a player what the next useful step is.

## Decisions (proposed)
1. **One year bar** replaces the Month stat: a 12-month strip (September → August) with the current month marked, the
   tournament week at its end, a marker for each seeded area's ready month (greens show when they will be ready), and the
   months left. Clicking it opens the Course stats panel. While the tournament runs it hands over to P02's tournament-day strip
   (T053).
2. **"What next": one line under the bar**, the most useful single step, chosen by a pure rule in this order: the guide's step
   (P06, while it runs) → a demotion warning → a seeded surface that will not be ready → the first missing item for the current
   tier → the first missing item for the next tier → money running low → "Ready: skip to the tournament". Each line has a Go
   that jumps to the place or opens the tool, like the results' Go buttons.
3. **Money by the month:** at each month turn, book that month's green fees, memberships (yearend.ts monthly functions) and
   course and road upkeep. The results then show the year's totals already booked, and only the tournament's own lines are
   added at `closeRound`. A month-turn toast is not needed; the treasury tooltip shows "this month: +$X".
4. **The tier offer is a choice:** when `verdict` offers a tier, the results card shows **Move up to <tier>** (the default) and
   **Stay at <tier>**. Staying keeps the tier and resets poor years, as accepting does. The offer comes back next year if the
   requirements still hold.
5. **Demotion is warned all year:** after one poor year, the what-next line and the results card say "One more poor year and the
   tour moves you down to <tier>", with the reputation needed.
6. **Skip checks the course first:** if a seeded surface will not be grown in by the tournament, Skip asks once: "Greens on
   holes 3 and 7 will not be ready (−N quality). Skip anyway / Lay sod (cost $X) / Cancel". Lay sod converts those areas to sod
   at the sod price.
7. **Tournament week as days, still resolved:** the results card gains a week strip: each practice day and each tournament
   day with its crowd, money and verdict line, the live day marked. Days other than the live one stay formulas (P02's decision);
   playing practice days live is Beta.

## Not in Alpha
- Practice days and days 2+ played live (Beta, with P11).
- New cards, new tier rules, or changing the tier thresholds (balance is P05).
- A calendar of town events, sponsors' visits, or a mid-season tournament.

## Risks
- Booking by the month changes when money arrives, so a careless early game can now go broke in spring rather than at the
  results. That is intended (GOLF-PLAN 2.1), but P05 must check the tier-1 start money against it.
- The what-next line competes with the guide (P06) and the first-run hints: the guide wins while it runs, and the old hints
  retire when T047 lands, so only one line ever shows.
- Old saves have no "last booked month": on load, treat the months already passed this season as booked, so nothing is paid
  twice.
