# P20: Every lot from a curated preset (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **decided by the lead under the owner's standing rule** (2026-10-24, recorded in docs/LEAD.md section 5). The owner: "I need every single preset residential/commercial and industrial in the lots so I can fine tune every single one"; the auto-generation "needs to stop"; the baked house lots "do not have right lighting or style".

## Where we are (verified in the code, 2026-10-24)
- **How a lot is drawn today.**
  - `lots/compose.ts compose(lot, st)` lays out every lot at runtime from its seed, use, level and decline: houses (`res`), shops (`com`), car parks (`park`), industrial yards (`ind`), tour shops and civic lots.
  - Nothing it composes is saved: a load composes every lot again.
- **The baked house lots** (the studio's Lots tab, `lot:8x15-front|rear-N`) are the only presets. There are 14 of them, one size (8x15), residential only. They are used by about 8% of a town's lots.
  - They are rendered from the **TrickOrTreat builder's old art** (`lot-render.mjs`): night lighting, pumpkins, the wrong style.
  - Their studio edits are keyed by placement index, so any change to the generator scrambles them.
  - Each takes a quarter of an atlas page.
  - **They are switched off since 2026-10-24** (`compose.ts BAKED_LOTS = false`): every house lot is composed from the game's art until this plan lands.
- **Lot sizes:** frontage lots are 10–20 tiles wide, depth 6–20. Block lots sit on a 0.5-tile grid. A town has about 156 distinct sizes, or **about 24 when rounded to 2 tiles**. Half the lots are flipped (road along x), and a quarter face backwards (road behind).

## Decisions
1. **A preset is a stored prop list plus ground patches, not a picture.**
   - Format: `src/game/lots/lot-presets.json`, read at runtime like `golf/hole-presets.json`.
   - Each prop is `{ id, x, y, scale, view }` in the lot's own frame:
     - x is along the street (0 at the left, as seen from the street);
     - y is away from the street;
     - view is the turn of the prop's art against the lot's facing (0 = the front faces the street).
   - Ground patches (drives, walks, car-park asphalt with bay lines, beds, yards) use the same frame.
   - A picture cannot turn, needs an exact size and costs atlas pages; a prop list turns with the four-view art and is edited without a rebuild.
2. **Every lot comes from a preset; no runtime layout.**
   - `compose` picks a preset by **use** (res, com, ind, tour, civic), **state** (built, upgraded, declined: decline 2 or more) and **size bucket** (w and d rounded to 2 tiles). With no preset for the bucket, it takes the nearest bucket of that use and state.
   - Among the presets of a bucket, the pick is by the lot's slot along its street, so neighbours differ.
   - Still procedural, because they are not one lot's to tune: the construction site (BUILDING), empty and vacant lots, the boundary fences on shared lines, the street items and laybys. The decline 1 and 3 touches stay too: fewer plants, a little junk.
3. **Fitting a preset to a lot of another size in its bucket:**
   - Props in the left half keep their distance from the left edge; props in the right half keep theirs from the right edge.
   - Props in the front half keep their distance from the street; the back half keeps its distance from the back line.
   - Anything that falls outside the lot is dropped.
4. **Facings:** a preset is authored facing the street on the +y side. The other three facings turn it, and each prop is drawn with the matching view of its four-view art (`views-library.ts`). **Nothing is ever mirrored** (mirror.ts). Lettered signs keep their single facing.
5. **The first presets are frozen from today's composer.**
   - Four variants per use, state and bucket, from fixed seeds, written once to `lot-presets.json`.
   - The owner's 14 curated house lots are converted to explicit presets for the 10x15 house bucket. Their layout and edits are applied; their houses are mapped to the game's new four-view houses; TrickOrTreat-only and Halloween sprites are dropped.
   - After that, `lot-presets.json` is the only source: the owner tunes it in the studio.
6. **The studio Lots tab edits the presets:**
   - It lists every preset by use, then size, then state.
   - It draws with the game's own atlas art, not TrickOrTreat's.
   - Edits: move, turn (view), bigger or smaller, delete, **add a sprite from the library**, **replace a sprite**, and duplicate a preset. Ground patches can be moved and resized.
   - Save writes the JSON at once. The dev game reloads it, with no 20-minute rebuild.
7. **The baked lot pipeline retires** once the conversion is checked: `lot-variants`, `lot-edits`, `lot-render`, their atlas pages, and the Lots tab's old view.

## The lot lab (2026-10-26, branch `lot-presets`): decision 6 as the owner asked for it
The owner: "at the presets page be able to generate a Lot preview with the building pointed at a road with the fence and everything like it would generate the lot in the game ... edit that generated lot, add stuff, delete and rotate, and save. then that lot becomes one that can generate in the game ... hundreds of possible lots on all the different road angles for every type". Then: it replaces the studio's old Lots tab, shows only one road and the lot beside it (never the town), picks the road angle and lot at random, paints asphalt and pavement, and farms are lots too.
- **Where:** the studio's **Lots** tab frames the game's own dev server at `?lotlab&dev` (`src/game/dev/lot-lab.ts`; the studio reads the game's URL from `STUDIO_GAME_URL`, default http://localhost:5174, which must run from the same checkout). So a lot is composed and drawn by the real game (`Game.stageLab`: an empty map, saving and farms off, the sim paused, one street at the road angle and one staged lot), masked to the lot and its road. Edits use the in-game dev editor (click, drag to move, R turn, Del, [ ], the sprite palette); ground is painted as rectangles of asphalt, paving, concrete, slabs, gravel or dirt.
- **Road angle:** `road` 0..7 on a preset, the side its street lies on in eighth turns (0 the street on map +y, screen bottom-left; odd values are the road tool's 45-degree diagonals). `preset-frame.ts roadOf(n)`; a diagonal preset is drawn with `quarterOfRoad` so a lot a few degrees off keeps its views.
- **Approval:** a lab preset (one with `road`) is a draft until `approved: true`; drafts are drawn only in the lab (`ComposeState.preset` forces a preset). `presets.ts approvedFor`: approved presets of the lot's own road at its size bucket (else within one bucket step, the nearest), else on an axis road those of another axis road (turned); a diagonal takes only its own. They **replace** the frozen presets for that lot: what the owner approves is what the town shows. On a diagonal road (a green before: buildings face the map axes) an approved preset of that road gives the lot its building.
- **Farms:** use `farm`. The lab composes the game's own farm (`composeFarm`) and stores it as a preset (`preset-from.ts presetFromComposition`: the freeze's footprint-centre and base-view conversion); a farm preset holds everything, fences included, and draws alone. The engine draws an approved farm preset on a parcel of its road (nearest size, no cap) instead of `composeFarm`.
- **Kept procedural on town lots** (decision 2 stands): fences, street items, leaf decals and decline touches. Shown in the lab, not editable per preset.
- **The file:** lab presets live in `lot-presets.json` with the frozen ones; a freeze keeps every lab preset as it is (`freeze-lot-presets.ts`). Tests: `tests/lot-presets-lab.test.ts`, `tests/lot-presets-farm.test.ts`.
- **Editing tools (T148, #309):** multi-select (Shift+click, a box on empty ground, Esc), the edits on the whole selection, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V
  (on this lot or another), Change house (H: the next of its family and size class, on its spot), the Lot grid (L, under the sprites),
  Generate in place (the lot kept, a new layout and building) beside New lot (the old random Generate), and Neighbours (N: the lots round it,
  for context, never edited). Details: `docs/handovers/lot-presets.md`.
- **T066 still open:** the old baked-lot routes in `server.mjs` and the scripts behind them are unused now; retire them with the atlas pages.

## Risks
- **Size fitting** at the edges of a bucket: props near the middle line may crowd. The owner can tune each bucket; the checks report props dropped by fitting.
- **Variety:** four variants per bucket may repeat along a long street. The owner can add more in the studio (duplicate, then change).
- **Draw cost:** a lot drawn from props has 20–60 sprites, as composed lots do today. That is no worse than now (only 8% of lots were baked).
