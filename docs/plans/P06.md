# P06: Year 1 onboarding, a guided first tournament (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **draft for the owner's approval** (second lead, 2026-10-24). Issue #69.

## Where we are (verified in the code)
- **T032/T033 progressive tools** (src/game/ext/progressive-ui): stages 1–3 hide rail groups, tools, golf chips, course brushes and fine controls, driven by the player's own saved counters (`GolfProgress`: pieces, roads, parks, structures, playS, dismissed, advanced). Hidden is not locked: hotkeys still work.
- **Hints:** 4 one-line `FIRST_RUN_HINTS`, picked by `nextFirstRunHint`, shown in the golf bottom bar with "Got it" (T035). Nothing on screen points at the tool a hint means, and the hints stop before the tournament.
- **A new golf game** already has 18 holes, a car park and town roads. So the guide teaches *adding to* a course, not building one from nothing.

## Decisions (proposed)
1. **A guided Year 1 of 7 steps.** Each step has a goal, a trigger that completes it (from saved facts, never from timers alone) and a target to point at.

   | # | Step | Done when | Points at |
   |---|------|-----------|-----------|
   | 1 | Welcome: your course, your town, the tournament at the end of August | "Next" | the month/countdown chip |
   | 2 | Lay a new hole: Course → Pieces: a tee, a fairway, a green | a tee, a fairway and a green piece placed (new `kinds` counter) | the Course rail tab, then the Pieces chip, then a map spot (below) |
   | 3 | Connect it: a cart path to the road | a cart path piece placed, or a road built | the Pieces list, the nearest road |
   | 4 | Room to park: a car park beside a road | parks ≥ 1 | the Transport tab, then the Car park tool |
   | 5 | Open the gates: an entrance gate from Event | structures ≥ 1 | the Event chip |
   | 6 | Play the tournament: Skip to tournament, and watch the crowd arrive | runs ≥ 1 (the first tournament done) | the Skip chip |
   | 7 | Results: read the verdict and plan Year 2 (every tool now unlocked) | "Got it" | the results panel |

2. **Pointing on screen.**
   - A UI target gets a pulsing gold ring: a `data-guide="<id>"` attribute on the rail tab, chip or button, and one CSS class.
   - A map target is a world point. The engine draws a pulsing ghost ring there (`drawGhostRing`, ext/ghost-preview), and the minimap shows a dot.
   - The step-2 spot is a free patch of owned land next to the course, found from `golf.parcel` and the painted areas (a pure helper). Steps 3 and 4 point at the nearest road segment to the clubhouse.
3. **The bar.** The golf bottom bar shows the current step: title, one line, "Next" (for steps that complete on Next), "Skip tutorial", and a step counter "2 / 7". It replaces the four first-run hints; their ids are kept as aliases so dismissals carry over.
4. **Returning players and Advanced.**
   - A save without `progress` (pre-T033) is an experienced player: no guide.
   - "Skip tutorial" sets `progress.tutorial = 'off'`. The gear menu has "Restart tutorial" (it sets `'on'` and resets the step), which doesn't touch the counters.
   - The guide's steps never hide or lock anything, and All tools (Advanced) doesn't stop the guide. They are independent switches.
   - Year 2 or later ends the guide.
5. **Rules stay pure.**
   - `guideStep(facts): GuideStep | null` in progressive-ui/guide.ts, with `GuideStep { id, n, title, body, done: 'next' | 'auto', ui?: string, map?: 'newHoleSpot' | 'nearestRoad' | null }`.
   - The facts are T033a's plus `kinds` (counts by piece kind), `runs`, `tutorial` and `step`.
   - All decisions live there and are unit-tested; the UI and the engine only render.

## Not in Alpha
- Voice-over, animated arrows and per-tool deep tutorials (the Junctions, traffic and budget panels).
- Tutorials for classic (city) mode.
