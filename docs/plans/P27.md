# P27: Iconic places on every map (a drive-in and a high school first)

Status: **decided by the standing rule** (2026-10-27; the owner pre-approves the recommendations). Issue #594 (T219), branch
`arena/T219-iconic-places` from main at `6b73587`. T219 builds sections 1 to 4 and the first two places; section 5 is later tickets.

The owner (2026-10-27): "can you custom build some iconic places for the map that we reuse each time like the drive in and a highschool etc".

## 1. What an iconic place is
- **One hand-built composite with one footprint**: a whole preset (P20/T155: it holds every prop, patch and decal it draws; the game adds
  nothing procedural to it), authored once and reused on every map. The drive-in is 30 x 20 tiles (60 x 40 m), the high school 50 x 35
  (100 x 70 m): far bigger than a town lot (at most 20 x 20), so a place is not a lot. It takes a rectangle of its own that covers the
  empty lots under it.
- **It faces its street.** Authored in the preset frame (x along the street, y away from it, the street on the canonical side), it is laid
  on whichever side of the street it was given by the frame's quarter turn: four-view art takes its turned view, everything else keeps its
  picture. **Turned, never mirrored.**
- **Axis streets only** for now: a 45-degree street would need every piece's screen-axis view (T154); none of the places' art has one yet.
- **No economy yet.** It is scenery with a name; later it may draw visitors (the businesses' visits, `business/visits.ts`).

## 2. How it is authored (data the owner can edit)
- **A small place file**, `src/game/lots/iconic-places.json`: per place its id, its name pattern (`{town} Drive-In`), the residents at which
  it opens, and its whole preset in the lot presets' own format (`use: 'civic'`, `whole: true`, `road: 0`, never `approved`, so no ordinary
  lot ever draws it). It is read with the preset reader (`preset-read.ts`), so a bad edit drops that place, never the game.
- It is kept **out of `lot-presets.json`** (the owner's lab file) so the lab's saves never carry it and the two files never clash.
- **Stand-ins**: a piece whose art is missing is a library prop named in the place's `standIns` (library id → the art it waits for); the map
  labels the first of each kind "Stand In for <name>". The missing art is `docs/GOLF-ART-LIST.md` section 11. The library's own drive-in
  screen (`variation:ref-building-drive-in-screen-…`) is not used: its footprint runs across its picture, so three of its four facings draw
  it off the lot; the blank golf leaderboard (four views) stands in for the screen until art 142 lands.
- **Next (a later ticket)**: the lot lab opens a place (`?lab&place=drive-in`), edits it like any whole preset and saves it back to the place
  file, not to `lot-presets.json`.

## 3. How the town places it (once per map, by seed, at its size)
- **When**: the engine checks after each town message. A place whose residents (the drive-in 1,500, the high school 3,000) the town has
  reached, and which this map has not placed yet, looks for a site. No site: it tries again later (every 30 s), never twice.
- **Where** (`lots/iconic.ts` `findSite`, pure): along every town street side (not the course drives or cart paths, not a bridge or tunnel)
  within 1,000 tiles of the town centre (a 50 x 35 school does not fit a 40-tile town block: it takes the town's edge), the footprint at
  the verge edge every 2 tiles. Each spot scores by its road's class (the main roads
  first: a large frontage near the main road), its nearness to the centre, and a jitter seeded by the map seed and the place id; the spots
  are tried best first and the first **free** one wins: no road, no built or building lot, no water, not the golf land, a business, a car
  park, painted ground or another place. Same map and town, same spot.
- **Kept**: the save holds each place as `[id, x, y, q]` (its front corner and quarter turn). The city worker gets the places' rectangles
  (`places` message) and treats them as the golf land: the town never builds there and its new roads keep off (`City.reserved`,
  `blocked`). The empty lots under a place draw nothing; the trees in its footprint go.

## 4. Name, label and card
- Named after the town: "Maple Falls Drive-In", "Maple Falls High" (a town not named yet: "The Drive-In", "The High School").
- A world label of the first rank (as the businesses' names) over its centre, from the district zoom in.
- Clicking any of its props (select tool) opens its card: the name, what it is, when it opened (residents), its size and its stand-ins.
