# P01: What Alpha 1.0 is (scope and exit criteria)

Status: **approved by the owner** (2026-10-24, all as recommended). Issue #64.

Alpha 1.0 is the first build a few outside people play from a new game to their third tournament without help from us. It is
the MVP of docs/LEAD.md (M1–M5) plus safe saves, a guided first year, and a build that loads. Beta adds breadth (taxis, rain,
more venues); Alpha is about one loop that works, explains itself, and does not break.

## Not in Alpha 1.0
- The after-MVP list of LEAD.md: taxis and hotel trips, drop-off zones, rain delays (G8), the golf estate and a second venue (G9),
  courtesy cars, staff and volunteers, the wayward-ball scene.
- Days 2+ of a tournament as movers (they stay resolved from day 1's measurements; P02).
- Final art everywhere. The art that exists is used; missing art may be a placeholder if it reads clearly at play zoom.
- Sound and music, localisation, achievements, a settings menu beyond the gear menu, distribution (Steam, itch.io, installers).

## Exit criteria (all must hold on the Alpha candidate commit)
1. **Checks:** `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `sim:gate`, `town:gate` and `fix:gate` pass; `npm run build` succeeds.
2. **Performance:** the benchmark meets GAME-PLAN 2.3 on the worker PC: frame ≤ 8 ms at 60 fps at street and district zoom,
   sim tick ≤ 25 ms per 100 ms with 10k cars, atlases ≤ 96 MB at dpr 2, a road edit ≤ 30 ms. Plus a tier-3 tournament day at
   its peak crowd inside the same frame and tick budgets.
3. **Saves:** a save is ≤ 2 MB compressed after three seasons; saving and loading mid-season and mid-tournament restores a
   working game; an unreadable save shows the toast and keeps the backup (P07).
4. **The first year:** a new player finishes the 7 guide steps and reaches the first results without reading anything outside
   the game (checked by the owner and at least one tester who has not seen the game).
5. **Three seasons:** a scripted campaign (`campaign.ts`, careful strategy) and a hand-played game both reach tier 2 by the
   third tournament with money left; a careless game ends at tier 1 or in debt, never in a stuck state (no dead ends: every
   screen has a way on).
6. **Results explain themselves:** each poor result names its cause (late arrivals and where they got stuck, full car parks,
   gate queues, long exits, poor viewing) and points at a place on the map or a tool.
7. **Stability:** a 30-minute session through a tournament shows no console errors and no unbounded memory growth.
8. **Loading:** the build's first screen is interactive within 10 s on the worker PC from a cold cache (P08 sets how).
9. **Owner sign-off** after playing the candidate.

## How we get there
- The order of work: M2 (in flight) → M3 (in flight) → M4 → M5 → P08. Saves and the guide run alongside on the laptop.
- When M4 lands, add a headless **alpha gate** (`npm run alpha:gate`): three scripted seasons through the real engine paths
  (not only the campaign formulas) that fail on a stuck state, a console error, or a save over 2 MB. It joins the other gates on
  the worker (ticket T067 in P04).
- The candidate is tagged `alpha-1.0-rc` on main; fixes go to main and the tag moves.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-24: all as recommended)
1. Testers: the owner plus two or three people who have not seen the game.
2. Balance tiers 1–3; tiers 4–5 only run without errors and inside the budgets.
3. Placeholder art is allowed where a picture is still missing, if it reads clearly at play zoom.
4. The single HTML file (a folder or a link) is enough for Alpha; installers come with the release candidate.
