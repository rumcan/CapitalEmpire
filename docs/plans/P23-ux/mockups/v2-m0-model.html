The course town: what lives where

Holes are the parks, paths and drives are the streets, course buildings are the houses. Each is one tool on the rail with one panel, built with the same anatomy. Phase 1 splits today's Course tool into these three and keeps the town's tools and Paint where they are.

Course

the park town you build
Holes (the parks)PHASE 1
New hole first (the camera frames it; Build is blocked only by real problems; its own undo). After hole 1: Shape by hand in 1 m cells with Brush or Run; your holes as badges. More options in sections: Shapes · Look (My looks, stripes, collars) · Planting · Hole · Plants · Ground.
Paths (the streets)PHASE 1
Cart paths and Drives tabs, Draw or Upgrade, prices a metre, built like roads. One Streets tool with Roads as a third tab: decision 7. Path points on greens, crossings: Phase 3.
Buildings (the houses)PHASE 1
The clubhouse card with its picture; its front snaps to a drive. The drive stopgap: Phase 2. Proposed drive and the Tournament tab: Phase 3 (T069b).
