## The design (the lead's, pre-approved; the owner may redirect any part)

### 1. You: create a character (T240)
- **New game:** a short "Who are you?" card with a name, one of 12 looks (the 3D people pipeline's A-pose pictures made for the player:
  men and women, ages 25-60, 1985 clothes), and a starting golf bag of skills. You start as a mid-handicap amateur (about 18).
- **In the world:** your character stands at the range from day one, beside the shack. You can click to send them (walk) to a spot, or
  to a person or a thing. Idle clips play while they wait: idle, a club twirl, watching the range.
- **The camera:** a "Me" button frames your character; following is optional.
- **Interaction:** click a person to talk (their portrait card opens, see 4), click a range piece to use it (buy a bucket, hit balls
  at a mat, putt on a practice green), click a building to inspect it (its set-the-scene picture, see 3).

### 2. Your golf: skills you practise, play and use (T241)
- **Skills:** driving, irons, short game, putting and nerve, each 0-100. Together they give your handicap, as the round model's skill
  input does today for the field.
- **Practise to improve:** a bucket at the range raises driving and irons; the putting green raises putting; the chipping green raises
  the short game. Gains shrink as a skill grows. A lesson from a teaching pro (P26) adds a bonus. It all takes game time and money.
- **Play:**
  - your everyday round (T208's tee sheet, a slot for you);
  - money matches (5);
  - the club championship you host each August (P4's owner in the field), which you can enter once your handicap is good enough;
  - later, the bigger tours.
- **The sim:** your skills feed the shot model exactly as a field golfer's do, so practice shows in your scores.

### 3. Inspect: set-the-scene pictures (T242)
- **Inspect anything with a picture:** the range, the shack (inside and out), the bays, the clubhouse tiers, each town business, a house,
  the video store, the diner and so on. It opens a large picture card: a nostalgic, painted 1985 America view of the place, outside or
  inside. It has a line of text written for that place (warm, specific, period), plus the place's numbers underneath.
- **Run-down and upgraded:** every place has a run-down picture and an upgraded one; the bigger places have one per stage. The card
  shows the one that matches the place's state now (the range's upgrades, the clubhouse tier, a lot's built, upgraded or declined
  state).
- **Focus:** the picture also opens when the guide (T239) or an event points at a place ("Set the scene: your range").
- **Art:** GOLF-ART-LIST section 13 lists every picture. These are single wide pictures, which is RUN's strength (one image, no turns).

### 4. People: portraits, jobs, handicaps, skills (T243)
- **Click a person** (a townsperson, a golfer, staff) to open their card: a portrait, name, age, job, where they live, their handicap
  and skills if they golf, whether they are a member, and their mood.
- **Portraits:** painted head-and-shoulders, 1985, about 48 archetypes by age, sex and job. Each person gets one deterministically from
  their seed, with a name from a period name list.
- **A prosperous town looks healthier (T245):** each archetype has a "hard times" and a "good times" portrait (worn clothes and tired
  faces against smart clothes and a healthy glow), shown by the town's prosperity: the club's success, T221's earnings and the lots'
  state. The 3D people get the same: a second, smarter clothing set for the townspeople models, chosen by the same rule.

### 5. Money matches (T244)
- **Challenge a person who golfs** from their card: match play over 9 or 18 holes for a stake you set (within what both can afford).
  Your character and theirs play with the round model on your course, watchable, or skipped to the result.
- **Rivals:** the people you play remember the result. A rival may challenge you back. Winning raises your reputation a little; losing
  lightens your wallet.

### 6. The tournament you play in (T246)
- **Enter your own club championship** once your handicap qualifies (P4's live play, now as your character with your skills).
- **The Mayor's Cup:** the town's yearly event, hosted by you with the mayor presenting. It is the first tournament your character can
  win, before the tours.

## T240 as built (2026-11-04, #704)
- **Code:** `src/game/golf/you.ts` (pure: the route, the walk, the keys, the verbs, the door, the save), the engine's `you*` methods (Walk mode,
  the hover, the click, the raster of what blocks, the movers and the marker), `src/ui/golf/YouCards.tsx` (Who are you?, talk, the first tee,
  the verb cursors). Saved in `GolfSave.player.you` (x, y, facing, look). Tests: `tests/golf-you.test.ts`. Shots: `scripts/dev/you-shots.mjs`.
- **Input:** Walk mode (the top bar's Walk chip, on by default) works with Select in hand and no side mode open: a click (no drag) walks you
  there or to the thing and uses it, a drag still pans, Ctrl+click is Select's old instant look; WASD / the arrows walk you (they pan the map
  when Walk is off), Shift runs. Me re-centres and follows again. A second click on the same place, or a double-click, runs; a trip over 60
  tiles shows Skip.
- **Pace:** you walk in real time (1.4 m/s, a jog 3.8 m/s) whatever the sim's speed (T320's slow and watching speeds included).
- **Route:** found once per click (offroad.ts gridPath over a raster of what blocks: buildings, the range's structures, iconic places, house
  lots, fences, parked cars, water); pavements and paths cheapest, roads dear (crossed, not walked; no crossing data is used yet), bunkers dear.
- **For T317:** `youClick`, `startWalk`/`routeOf`, `youSkip` (Jump to ball), the first tee's card (`youTeeOpen`, `youTeeView`) and the keys'
  `keyStep` are the hooks: snap-to-address is a `startWalk` to the address point when within reach.

## T317 as built (2026-11-04, #903): play a round as yourself
- **Code:** `src/game/golf/play/yours.ts` (pure: the hole choice, the round and its scorecard, `playYours`, the snap distance and the address, next hole,
  the flight's timing, the save), `live.ts shotOutcome` (LiveGroup.play's shot, shared: the tournament's shots are unchanged), `you.ts` (`You.speed`: the
  cart is a You at `CART_TILES`; `YouSave.cart`), `PlayerProfile.round` (saved whole: a reload comes back at your ball), the engine's T317 section
  (the cart, the snap, `yourHit`/`yourCaddie`/`yourJump`/`yourNextHole`/`yourRoundEnd`, the ball's flight and the camera), `src/ui/golf/YourRound.tsx`
  (the tee card's hole choice, the round bar, the scorecard). Tests: `tests/golf-yours.test.ts`. Shots: `scripts/dev/round-shots.mjs --headed`
  (a headless Edge's GPU often fails here: the ground does not draw).
- **How to play:** your golf cart waits beside where you first stand (the clubhouse). Click it (or E beside it) to get in; WASD / the arrows drive
  (Shift faster), a click drives there on a route, E gets out. The cart keeps off greens, bunkers, water and buildings. At the first tee its card asks
  "Play all N / the front 9 / the back N-9 / Pick holes". Walk or drive up to the ball: within 8 m you snap to the address and the shot panel opens
  (the T316 meter). After the shot the ball flies (the camera follows), then you walk or drive to it, or press **Jump to ball**. Holed (or picked up at
  par + 5), the scorecard opens: "Next hole", and after the last "Done" puts the round into your rounds (an everyday round: handicap and nerve).
- **Decisions:** your round is a walk-on played in real time, not on the day's playback: the everyday groups neither wait for you nor hold you (pace
  with the field needs T320's watching speed; a follow-up). A round booked today and not yet under way is replaced by the round you play by hand. The sim
  is not paused at your address (the tournament's owner shot still pauses). You play the white tees and your everyday game (skills at everyday pressure).
  "Let the caddie finish the hole" plays the rest of that hole by the model.
- **For T319:** `youHoledOut(row, up)` is where the ball's visible drop into the cup goes; the flight is `yrFlight` (play/yours.ts `YourFlight`,
  `yourBallAt`). **For T318:** shot tracers and the spin ball can read `yrFlight` too.

## T323 as built (2026-11-04, #921): Walk and Select cursors, no camera follow
- **Code:** `src/game/golf/cursors.ts` (pure: the remembered mode, the lone Alt tap, the walk and pan keys, the camera rule, what a click does), the
  engine's `youKeysOn`/`setYouWalk`/`toggleYouWalk`, `src/ui/Rail.tsx` `CursorPair`. Tests: `tests/golf-cursors.test.ts`. In-game checks and shots:
  `scripts/dev/cursor-shots.mjs --headed`.
- **Input:** the rail's Select slot is two cursors: the pointer (Select: a click inspects) and the walking figure (Walk: a click walks you there and
  uses what you clicked; Ctrl+click looks at once). A lone tap of **Alt** swaps them (keyup default prevented: the browser menu bar never takes the
  focus; Alt+key, Alt+click, Alt+Tab and typing never swap). The mode is remembered (localStorage `capitalempire.cursorMode`, Walk by default).
  The top-bar Walk chip is gone; Me stays. **WASD** walk you (or drive the cart; E in/out) in either cursor with Select in hand; the **arrows pan** the
  camera (WASD pan too when you have no character or another tool is in hand).
- **Camera:** never follows you walking or driving (Me centres once). It still follows your ball's flight after a shot (that is the shot; a drag
  of the map lets go).
