## The design (the lead's, pre-approved; the owner may redirect any part)

### 1. You: create a character (T240)
- **New game:** a short "Who are you?" card with a name, one of 12 looks (the 3D people pipeline's A-pose pictures made for the player:
  men and women, ages 25-60, 1985 clothes), and a starting golf bag of skills. You start as a mid-handicap amateur (about 18).
- **In the world:** your character stands at the range from day one, beside the shack. You can click to send them (walk) to a spot, or
  to a person or a thing. Idle clips play while they wait: idle, a club twirl, watching the range.
- **The camera:** a "Me" button frames your character; following is optional.
- **Interaction:** click a person to talk (their portrait card opens, see 4), click a range piece to use it (buy a bucket, hit balls
  at a mat, putt on a practice green), click a building to inspect it (its set-the-scene picture, see 3).

### 2. Your golf: skills you practise, play and use (T241)
- **Skills:** driving, irons, short game, putting and nerve, each 0-100. Together they give your handicap, as the round model's skill
  input does today for the field.
- **Practise to improve:** a bucket at the range raises driving and irons; the putting green raises putting; the chipping green raises
  the short game. Gains shrink as a skill grows. A lesson from a teaching pro (P26) adds a bonus. It all takes game time and money.
- **Play:**
  - your everyday round (T208's tee sheet, a slot for you);
  - money matches (5);
  - the club championship you host each August (P4's owner in the field), which you can enter once your handicap is good enough;
  - later, the bigger tours.
- **The sim:** your skills feed the shot model exactly as a field golfer's do, so practice shows in your scores.

### 3. Inspect: set-the-scene pictures (T242)
- **Inspect anything with a picture:** the range, the shack (inside and out), the bays, the clubhouse tiers, each town business, a house,
  the video store, the diner and so on. It opens a large picture card: a nostalgic, painted 1985 America view of the place, outside or
  inside. It has a line of text written for that place (warm, specific, period), plus the place's numbers underneath.
- **Run-down and upgraded:** every place has a run-down picture and an upgraded one; the bigger places have one per stage. The card
  shows the one that matches the place's state now (the range's upgrades, the clubhouse tier, a lot's built, upgraded or declined
  state).
- **Focus:** the picture also opens when the guide (T239) or an event points at a place ("Set the scene: your range").
- **Art:** GOLF-ART-LIST section 13 lists every picture. These are single wide pictures, which is RUN's strength (one image, no turns).

### 4. People: portraits, jobs, handicaps, skills (T243)
- **Click a person** (a townsperson, a golfer, staff) to open their card: a portrait, name, age, job, where they live, their handicap
  and skills if they golf, whether they are a member, and their mood.
- **Portraits:** painted head-and-shoulders, 1985, about 48 archetypes by age, sex and job. Each person gets one deterministically from
  their seed, with a name from a period name list.
- **A prosperous town looks healthier (T245):** each archetype has a "hard times" and a "good times" portrait (worn clothes and tired
  faces against smart clothes and a healthy glow), shown by the town's prosperity: the club's success, T221's earnings and the lots'
  state. The 3D people get the same: a second, smarter clothing set for the townspeople models, chosen by the same rule.

### 5. Money matches (T244)
- **Challenge a person who golfs** from their card: match play over 9 or 18 holes for a stake you set (within what both can afford).
  Your character and theirs play with the round model on your course, watchable, or skipped to the result.
- **Rivals:** the people you play remember the result. A rival may challenge you back. Winning raises your reputation a little; losing
  lightens your wallet.

### 6. The tournament you play in (T246)
- **Enter your own club championship** once your handicap qualifies (P4's live play, now as your character with your skills).
- **The Mayor's Cup:** the town's yearly event, hosted by you with the mayor presenting. It is the first tournament your character can
  win, before the tours.

  "Let the caddie finish the hole" plays the rest of that hole by the model.
- **For T319:** `youHoledOut(row, up)` is where the ball's visible drop into the cup goes; the flight is `yrFlight` (play/yours.ts `YourFlight`,

## T318 as built (2026-11-04, #904): spin control, the ball in the bottom right
- **Model** `src/game/golf/play/spin.ts`: `SpinInput { x, y, pump }` (the dot on the ball: x -1 draw .. +1 fade, y -1 backspin .. +1 topspin; pump the
  clicks in the air). `spinEffect`: the dot gives up to `SPIN.base` (0.5), each click `SPIN.per` (0.1), capped by `spinCap`: backspin by club
  (`SPIN_BACK_CAP`: driver 0.12, 3-wood 0.2, 4-iron 0.35, 6-iron 0.5, 8-iron 0.7, PW 0.9, SW 1; putter none), every spin by the lie it is played from
  (`SPIN_LIE`: rough 0.45, fescue 0.25, bunker 0.6). `shot.ts hitShot` `HitMod.spin`: side spin curves the flight (`curveOf`, up to 6 degrees at 200 m)
  at the strike, with no extra draws (the stream is the same); from the landing, `spinRollM`: topspin runs on (x 2.5 at full), backspin checks and past
  1/1.6 spins back up to `BACK_M` (green 6 m, fairway 2.5 m, rough 0.4 m); side spin kicks the bounce (14 degrees at full).
- **The T319 split:** `Landing.spin` (the spin's change to the roll, m; the `spinRoll` hook) and `Landing.check` (a spin back's skid point) are exposed
  for T319's roll; `roll.ts` is untouched (the field's BallRoll runs land to rest, so a spin back rolls straight back). Your round's ball
  (`yours.ts yourBallAt`) hops on to the check, then zips back.
- **Saved:** `OwnerInput.spin` (live.ts), a full shot only; absent = no spin, so saves from before T318 replay exactly. `shotOutcome` returns `spin`.
- **In the air:** the clicks change only the roll, never the landing. At the landing (engine `spinLand`): your round plays the shot again from the round
  before it with the clicks (`playYours`); the tournament rebuilds the day from the saved inputs, the last with its clicks (`buildPlay`, what a reload
  does; about 12 ms once per spun shot with clicks; none without), keeping the day's latches (`finish`, `waves`). PlayView `ballOf().air` says when the
  owner's ball is in the air.
- **UI** `src/ui/SpinBall.tsx` (bottom right, App mounts it on `game.spinView()`): click or drag the dot before the shot (right-click: none); in the air
  click the ball or press **F** (S walks, Space boosts the swing; F's flow overlay waits while the ball flies) for more, up to the cap (the meter's gold
  mark). Engine: `setShotSpin`, `spinView`, `spinPump`.
- Checks: `tests/golf-play-spin.test.ts`; `scripts/dev/spin-shots.mjs` (your round: the ball, a wedge spinning back), `scripts/dev/spin-tourney.mjs`
  (the tournament: clicks in the air, the rebuild's time).

## T319 as built (2026-11-04, #905): the break grid, shot tracers, the ball into the cup
- **Code:** `src/game/golf/play/green.ts` (pure: the break grid from the course height, the cup's speed window `cupFate`, `PuttRoll`, the tracer's curve
  `shotPoint`/`shotBend`/`shotShape` and life `tracerAlpha`, the two settings), `yours.ts` (`YourFlight` gains `aim`, `putt`, `hold`, `spin`; `flightOf`'s
  8th argument is now an options object `{ aim, putt, check, spin }`, `flightSpin(landing)` fills T318's part; `rollShare(u, spinM)`; `cupTime`), the engine's
  T319 section (`drawBreakGrid`, `drawYourTracers`, `drawPuttLine`, the ball's look, `cupSound`). Settings (gear): **Break grid** Off / Putting (default) /
  Always, **Shot tracers** (on). Tests `tests/golf-green.test.ts`; shots `scripts/dev/green-shots.mjs --headed` (the window opens off-screen).
- **The model stays the truth:** a holed putt's way ends in the cup, reaching it at sqrt(2 x 0.65 x 0.35) = 0.67 m/s (window 1.3 m/s dead centre, narrowing
  to 0 at the rim); a miss whose way crosses the cup lips out when it is there too fast (hop, "Lipped out!", a rattle), else its way is eased to shave the rim
  (it never drops when the model says it missed). The way bends with the break (roll.ts `BallRoll.line`) but ends where the model says. Saves unchanged.
- **Look:** the grid is drawn on the 2D overlay (no ground re-bake, no atlas page): a 1 m mesh and 2 m arrows downhill with a running dot, white (flat) /
  cyan / yellow / orange by slope, kept off cart paths, fading in when you putt. Tracer: yellow flight from the ball up to it, its ground track dashed, held
  2.5 s after landing then faded 1.5 s; at most 3. The ball: a little bigger with a 145-degree shadow; in the cup a dark oval with its top showing.
- **Cost:** the grid's draw 0.09-0.16 ms a frame while shown (measured in the game, one green), nothing when hidden; tracers 3 polylines of 40 points.
- **Not done:** tracers for the field's balls (the playback has no per-ball flight record; optional in the brief). The grid draws over the golfer (overlay).

## T325 as built (2026-11-04, #925): mulligans in your own round
- **Settings:** the first tee's card has a row **Mulligans: None / 1 per nine (default) / Unlimited** (`YourRound.rules`, `play/yours.ts MULL_RULES`).
  A round from before it (no `rules`) has none. `rulesFor(kind)` gives a competition (the tournament, a money match) none whatever is chosen: your
  round (`yours.ts`) is the only place mulligans exist, and the tournament owner's shot (`live.ts`) never reaches it.

  offset by `mk`, so the same swing is not the same outcome; `mk` absent keeps old saves' streams). The engine drops the flight, its tracer and the
  spin in the air, and opens the shot again on the same club, aim and power (`yrLastInput`).
- **Per nine:** one in holes 1-9 of the round, one in 10-18 (`mulligansLeft`).
- **Scorecard:** a small "m" by the hole's score, "· 2 mulligans" in the total, and "Counts in your rounds, not toward your handicap". At the end
  `noHandicapWhy` goes to `playedRound(..., noHcp)`: the round is in your rounds (marked `noHcp`) and your nerve does not rise.
- **Saved:** the round is saved whole (T317), now with `rules`, `mk`, the rows' `mull` and `undo`: a reload rebuilds the same round and the same next

## T244 as built (2026-11-04, #706): money matches
- **Code:** `src/game/golf/play/match.ts` (pure: the opponent, stakes, the answer, match-play scoring, the opponent's shots, golf's order, skip, settle,
  rivals, return challenges, the save), `yours.ts` (`YourRound.mode` shared with T246: `'casual' | 'championship' | 'mayors-cup' | 'match'`, copied
  verbatim from T246's branch; `YourRound.match`), `player.ts` (`rivals`, `challenge`, `chDay`), the engine's T244 section (`matchOffer`,
  `challengeMatch`, `matchBegin`, `matchFrame`/`matchOppHit`, `matchSkip`, `matchSettle`, `matchDaily`, `answerChallenge`, `drawMatchBall`, the
  opponent's figure in `youMovers`), `src/ui/golf/MatchCards.tsx` (the challenge box, the match strip, the scorecard's match rows, the result card, the
  return challenge). Tests: `tests/golf-match.test.ts`. Shots: `scripts/dev/match-shots.mjs --headed` (off-screen).
- **How to play:** walk up to a person who golfs (everyone at the range or on the course, about half the townspeople): their card (the talk card, a
  stand-in until T243's portraits: the hook is `youTalk.hcp`) has **Money match**: their handicap and yours, 9 or 18 holes (what the course has), a
  stake slider from $10 to what both can afford (your money, their purse). **Challenge**: they say yes or no (a line). Yes: the match is your round
  on the first n holes in mode 'match', your cart brought to the first tee, the opponent on the tee. You play your shots as in your round (the meter,
  the cart, Jump to ball); they play theirs by the round model when they are away (golf's order: the honour on the tee, then the one farther from the
  pin), their figure swings at their ball and their ball flies (the camera follows it). The round bar gets the match strip (vs whom, the stake,
  "2 up thru 7" / "Dormie 2", each hole's dot, whose turn, **Skip to result**); Quit becomes **Concede** (you lose the stake).
- **Decisions:** level match play, no handicap strokes (said on the card). Your shots at a match's pressure (`PRESSURE.match`); no mulligans and no
  gimmies (`rulesFor('match')`); the opponent's game is their handicap's skills at the same pressure. A hole is decided once neither side can do
  better (the other already has as many strokes without holing: picked up, marked * on the card); the match at 3&2 and so on. The opponent's figure
