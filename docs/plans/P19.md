# P19: Course roads, cart paths, and buildings that snap to them (Alpha 1.0)

Status: **decided by the lead under the owner's standing rule** (2026-10-24, recorded in docs/LEAD.md section 5). The owner: the golf course needs "pavement roads for golf carts and single fancy lane roads for cars"; the fancy roads "can start as gravel but have its own upgrade tier"; the golf buildings snap to both; "you should be able to build these paths the same way as the game builds roads".

## Where we are (verified in the code, 2026-10-24)
- **Cart paths are painted rectangles.**
  - The start's loop: `golf/dressing.ts buildCartPathLoop`, 5 m pieces with surface `'path'`.
  - The Course > Build "Cart path" brush, and a `path-stub` piece that snaps to a road kerb.
  - There are no joins and no graph.
- **Roads** are one `RoadNetwork` with a table-driven catalogue (`network.ts` PLAIN, VARIANTS, TYPES).
  - The road tool draws 90° or 45° polylines with round joins, snaps (`snap.ts`), splits crossings into junctions, and charges by length.
  - Every seg gets traffic lanes, can grow town lots (`segLots`), and pays upkeep.
  - The upgrade ladder is the flat `WIDER` / `NARROWER` maps.
- **Nothing snaps to a road.**
  - Business placement refuses any road within 2 tiles (`bizWhy`).
  - Event structures ignore roads.
  - The four-view art could face a building any of 4 ways.

## Decisions
0. **The course roads are a miniature of the town's road catalogue** (the owner, 2026-10-24: "the roads on the golf course is just a miniature version of the ones we have in town; we should still be able to make avenue style paths where there's trees or gardens in the middle"). Each kind has its tiers and its variants, as town roads do: plain, with trees along the sides, and avenue styles with a median of trees, grass or a garden (shrubs and flower beds, a new median flag `gd`).
1. **Cart paths are a second road network: the path network.**
   - It reuses the road tool's drawing, snapping, joins and route rules, and the ground painter.
   - It has no traffic lanes, no town lots and no upkeep beyond its own.
   - A path crossing a road draws over it with a **cart crossing** (no junction): GOLF-PLAN's crossing.
   - The path classes join the catalogue as a new family, `cart`:
     - **Gravel cart path**, 2.5 m (the cheap start);
     - **Cart path**, paved 2.5 m, with or without trees along the sides;
     - **Avenue cart path**: a path each way either side of a median of trees, grass or a garden.
   - The ladder runs gravel → paved → avenue.
2. **The course drive is a new road type in the main network** (cars use it), family `course`, with its own tiers and ladder:
   - **Gravel drive:** a single lane, 4 m, gravel.
   - **Paved drive:** a single lane, kerbed, with or without trees along the sides.
   - **Avenue drive:** one lane each way either side of a median of trees, grass (`gr`) or a garden (`gd`).
   - Course drives never grow town lots (a `noLots` field read by `segLots`) and never step up into a town street (their own `WIDER` / `NARROWER` entries).
   - **Golf tier gates the tiers:** gravel from the start, paved from tier 2, avenue from tier 3.
   - The start's gravel drive to the clubhouse becomes a Gravel drive.
   - A brick or cobble surface and garden median art go on the art queue. Until then the garden median uses the yard shrubs and flowers.
3. **Golf buildings snap to drives and cart paths.**
   - When a golf business plot (clubhouse, pro shop, cart garage, halfway pavilion) or an event structure (tent, stand, gate, toilets) is placed within 6 tiles of a course drive or cart path, its front edge sits on the verge.
   - It faces the road with the matching one of its four views; never mirrored.
   - R turns the building through its four views when nothing is in reach.
   - The 2-tile road refusal becomes "touching the verge is fine, overlapping the road is not".
4. **Building them:** Course > Build gets a **Paths** group: the three cart paths and the three drives, with the road tool's click, preview, snap and price. The drives also show in the Roads palette under a "Course" header in golf games only.

## Risks
- **T068 touches the engine's road tool, the ground workers and the save together.** It goes after T067 (both touch network.ts and engine.ts), in one worktree, with a browser check of road drawing before it lands.
- **Occupancy:** each network has its own occupancy grid. Props and buildings must test both, or a tree may stand on a path.
- **Old saves** have no `paths`: they load with an empty path network, and their painted cart-path areas stay as decoration.
