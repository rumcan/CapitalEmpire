# Later-era modest house geometry references

Technical only: four fixed world models, eight views each. These are geometry references for native painted sprite generation, never final game art or runtime registrations.

Read [brief-index.json](./brief-index.json) for every design, dimensions, exact doors/features, source mesh and paired normal/yaw8 calibration files. Normal cells are SE/SW then NW/NE; yaw8 cells are N/E then S/W. Features live at fixed metre coordinates and rotate with one model; their rear sides do not acquire front doors.

Final art keeps the established painterly town style with late-afternoon daylight from the upper right, transparent background and no ground plate, labels, people or cars. Houses represent gradual ordinary renewal from the existing1980s town through1990s,2000s,2010s and2020s. No mansions or futuristic forms.
